# Usability Testing
The following are the results from the usability survey that was deployed during the testing day held on 2nd of February. The total participant number was 28 and the required consent form was filled with signatures. 

### Score Explanation
Across 16 questions, users were asked to select from a scale of 1 to 7, where 1 is "Strongly Agree" and 7 is "Strongly Disagree." The scores were then aggregated per question to identify positive and negative feedback. Each question is part of a category; aggregating questions from the same category highlights specific areas of the product needing improvement.

### The two highest scored answers (nearest to 1)
 - I reckon that I could complete future tasks (bookings/approvals) using this website.
 - The interface of this website was pleasant, especially the interactive map in the booking page.

### The two lowest scored answers (nearest to 7)
 - Whenever I made a mistake using the system, I could recover easily and quickly.
 - The information (such as online help, on-screen messages, and other documentation) provided with this website was clear

### PSSUQ Scores
 - **System Usefulness**: 1.83

 - **Information Quality**: 2.11

 - **Interface Quality**: 1.68

 - **Overall**: 1.93
