# First Client Meeting Agenda

## 1. Current Procedures and Architecture

- **How does the current service operate?**
- **What are the arising problems?**
- **What are the flaws and advantages of the current processes?**
- **How does the company currently adhere to GDPR?**

## 2. Identifying Project Scope

- **Who are the intended users?**
- **What is the extent of certain features?** (e.g., Map functionality, invoicing system)
- **Does the mobile app need to be natively implemented or can it be web-based?**

## 3. Client Requirements and Demands

- **What features should our project implement?**
- **What specific problems does the client want to tackle?**
- **How detailed should the required features be?**

## 4. Client Resources and Contribution

- **What resources will the company provide?** (e.g., driver database, existing data, APIs, etc.)
- **What level of access will we have to current systems?**

## 5. Communication Protocol

- **Decide on the standard platform for communication** (email, Slack, Teams, etc.)
- **Determine meeting frequency and client availability**
- **Establish preferred communication channels for different types of updates**

## 6. Contract Discussion

- **Present contract options to the client**
- **Determine which option is preferred by the client**
- **Review and sign contract if time permits**

## 7. Next Steps and Follow-up

- **Schedule next meeting at a suitable time for both parties**
- **Define deliverables and timeline for next meeting**
- **Assign action items and responsibilities**
